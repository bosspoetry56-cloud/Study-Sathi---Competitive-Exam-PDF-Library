🧮 Mathematics Mock Test

10 Questions • 15 Minutes • Free Practice Test

1. Percentage

To pass an examination, a candidate needs 36% marks. A student scores 190 marks and fails by 35 marks. What is the maximum marks for the examination?

2. Profit, Loss & Discount

A shopkeeper marks an article 40% above its cost price and allows a discount of 20% on the marked price. What is his overall profit percentage?

3. Ratio & Proportion

Two numbers are in the ratio 3 : 5. If 9 is subtracted from each number, the ratio becomes 12 : 23. What is the smaller number?

4. Problems on Ages

The ratio of the present ages of a father and his son is 5 : 2. Four years hence, the ratio of their ages will be 11 : 5. What is the present age of the father?

5. Time & Work

A can complete a piece of work in 15 days and B can complete the same work in 20 days. They work together for 4 days, and then A leaves. In how many more days will B alone finish the remaining work?

6. Pipe & Cistern

Pipe A can fill a tank in 12 hours and Pipe B can fill it in 15 hours. Pipe C can empty the full tank in 20 hours. If all three pipes are opened simultaneously, how much time will it take to fill the tank completely?

7. Time, Speed & Distance

If a person travels at a speed of 30 km/h, he reaches his office 10 minutes late. If he increases his speed to 40 km/h, he reaches 5 minutes early. What is the distance between his home and office?

8. Train Problems

A 180 m long train is running at a uniform speed of 54 km/h. How many seconds will it take to completely cross a platform that is 320 m long?

9. Boat & Stream

The speed of a boat in still water is 10 km/h and the speed of the stream is 2 km/h. The boat travels 24 km upstream and returns 24 km downstream. What is the total time?

10. Simple & Compound Interest

The difference between compound interest and simple interest on a certain sum at 10% per annum for 2 years is ₹65. What is the principal sum?

📖 Detailed Solutions

1. Percentage — Answer: C) 625

Passing marks = 190 + 35 = 225.
36% of Maximum Marks = 225.
Maximum Marks = 225 × 100 / 36 = 625.

2. Profit, Loss & Discount — Answer: B) 12%

Let CP = 100.
Marked Price = 140.
After 20% discount, SP = 140 × 80/100 = 112.
Profit = 112 − 100 = 12.
Therefore Profit% = 12%.

3. Ratio & Proportion — Answer: B) 33

Numbers = 3x and 5x.
(3x − 9)/(5x − 9) = 12/23.
23(3x − 9) = 12(5x − 9).
69x − 207 = 60x − 108.
9x = 99 → x = 11.
Smaller number = 3 × 11 = 33.

4. Problems on Ages — Answer: B) 40 years

Present ages = 5x and 2x.
Four years later:
(5x + 4)/(2x + 4) = 11/5.
25x + 20 = 22x + 44.
3x = 24 → x = 8.
Father's age = 5 × 8 = 40 years.

5. Time & Work — Answer: B) 10⅔ days

A's one-day work = 1/15.
B's one-day work = 1/20.
Together = 7/60.
Work in 4 days = 28/60 = 7/15.
Remaining work = 8/15.
B takes (8/15) ÷ (1/20) = 10⅔ days.

6. Pipe & Cistern — Answer: B) 10 hours

Net rate = 1/12 + 1/15 − 1/20.
LCM = 60.
Net rate = 5/60 + 4/60 − 3/60 = 6/60 = 1/10.
Therefore tank fills in 10 hours.

7. Time, Speed & Distance — Answer: B) 30 km

Difference in arrival time = 15 minutes = 1/4 hour.
D/30 − D/40 = 1/4.
D(1/120) = 1/4.
Therefore D = 30 km.

8. Train Problems — Answer: C) 33⅓ seconds

Total distance = 180 + 320 = 500 m.
54 km/h = 15 m/s.
Time = 500/15 = 33⅓ seconds.

9. Boat & Stream — Answer: B) 5 hours

Upstream speed = 10 − 2 = 8 km/h.
Downstream speed = 10 + 2 = 12 km/h.
Upstream time = 24/8 = 3 hours.
Downstream time = 24/12 = 2 hours.
Total = 5 hours.

10. Simple & Compound Interest — Answer: C) ₹6,500

For 2 years at 10%, difference between CI and SI = P × (10/100)².
65 = P/100.
Therefore P = ₹6,500.
